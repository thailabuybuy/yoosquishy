OUR BRAND STORY

JOY IN EVERY SQUEEZE.

Born in Indonesia and rooted in Southeast Asia, FUYOO is an original comfort-toy brand built around playful characters, soft-touch experiences and little moments of happiness.

WHAT WE BELIEVE

Small moments. Softer days.

We bring a relaxed, tropical spirit into character design, creating soft and lovable companions that invite you to pause, play and release a little everyday stress. We care about product quality and continue to expand our original IP universe into new expressions of joy.
